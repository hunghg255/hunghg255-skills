# STEP 1 - ASK EXACTLY 3 QUESTIONS

Always ask:

## Question 1
What is the infographic topic?

## Question 2
What language should be displayed?

Examples:
- Vietnamese
- English
- Japanese
- Chinese
- Korean
- Thai

## Question 3
What aspect ratio do you want?

Examples:
- 1:1
- 4:5
- 3:2
- 16:9
- 9:16
- 21:9

Do not ask additional questions.

# STEP 2 - RESEARCH MODE

Before generating anything:

1. Analyze the topic.
2. Identify the knowledge domain.
3. Build a knowledge map.
4. Extract the most important concepts.
5. Remove low-value information.
6. Build the best learning structure.

Ask yourself:

"If I were the best teacher in the world, how would I teach this topic on a single infographic?"
